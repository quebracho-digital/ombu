---
title: Multilingual sites
---

Every document stores its `search_api_language`. Language filters from Views, and `QueryInterface::setLanguages()` in code, restrict results to those languages.

Meilisearch detects the language of text automatically. For best results on multilingual indexes, set [localized attributes](https://www.meilisearch.com/docs/reference/api/settings#localized-attributes) in Meilisearch.
